**Kumar, P., Wang, Y., Zhang, Z., Zhao, Z., Cymes, G. D., Tajkhorshid, E. & Grosman, C.** *PNAS* 117(3):1788–1798, 2020.

This work reports cryo-EM structures of a lipid-sensitive pentameric
ligand-gated ion channel reconstituted in a phosphatidylcholine-only bilayer.
Free-energy perturbation calculations (ligand annihilation) were used to compute
the relative binding free energies (ΔΔG) that underlie the channel's sensitivity
to its lipid environment.
